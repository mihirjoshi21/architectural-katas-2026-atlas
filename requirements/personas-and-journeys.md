# People and success measures

These are role-based personas, not evidence of interviews or named staff members.

| Role | Work and friction | Useful result |
|---|---|---|
| Countess / estate owner | Decide where to invest while improving contribution and repeat visits | Comparable zone/service trends, cost assumptions and uncertainty visible. |
| Visitor / family | Buy a suitable pass, enter, plan a day, find accessible activities | Clear entitlement and price; staffed/no-phone route; advice respects closures and stale information. |
| Gate agent | Admit valid parties while handling weak connectivity, exceptions and refunds | Local redemption authority, clear decisions and auditable exceptions. |
| Keeper | Record offered/consumed food and health; track animal/group populations | Structured fast capture, actionable evidence, manageable review queue. |
| Veterinarian / authorized care reviewer | Review consequential interventions and outcomes | Task-specific approval, context, traceable revision and escalation. |
| Mechanic | Perform inspections and maintenance on historic rides | Advisory measurements and work orders; maintenance authority preserved. |
| Operations manager | Allocate staff and respond to missing data or incidents | Popularity and backlog/freshness views connected to a specific action. |

Primary journeys: family purchase/entry; keeper feeding/observation/review; population reconciliation; visitor planning with unavailable live data; manager changing staff allocation. Detailed behavior is in [workflows](../design/workflows.md). Outcomes need seasonal baseline measurement; no improvement percentage is asserted as achieved.

The return journey is explicit: approved public information → checkout → visit → optional saved interests/return plan → separately permitted reminder → later admission. [Growth and measurement](../design/visitor-growth-and-popularity.md). Anonymous popularity counters do not identify returning visitors.
