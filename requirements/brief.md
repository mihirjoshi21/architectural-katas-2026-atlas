# Business brief

Von Digitalis Estates operates 40 rides and cares for over 200 animals across 55 displays/enclosures. Average attendance is 5,000 visitors per day, with an aspiration to reach at least 15,000 within three years.

The estate needs tickets and family passes; popularity information to guide staffing and investment; animal health and feeding observations; piranha population monitoring; increased return visits and profitability. Wi-Fi coverage is patchy. Cloud use requires a dependable transmission approach, and budget exists for MQTT-capable devices.

The design covers one estate. Jurisdiction, available staff, current applications, useful sensor placement, outage duration, budget ceiling and representative model labels require discovery. Existing domain safety and emergency procedures continue during digital modernization.
